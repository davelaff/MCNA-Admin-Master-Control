# Penetration Testing

**SSK Controls:** 07-6  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Biennial minimum; risk-triggered otherwise

MCNA conducts penetration tests on a risk-based schedule, at minimum once every two years for external-facing infrastructure. Tests are scoped by the Director of IT & MIS and executed by a qualified third-party firm engaged through DIS or independently procured. Scope typically covers external network perimeter, publicly accessible services, and social engineering scenarios.

Test findings are reviewed by the Director of IT & MIS. Critical and high findings are prioritized in the vulnerability management process and remediated per standard SLOs. All findings and remediation status are retained in the IT & MIS SharePoint library.

Any testing against Azure or Microsoft 365 infrastructure follows Microsoft's penetration testing rules of engagement and requires advance notification through the Microsoft Penetration Testing portal. Internal red team exercises are evaluated on a risk-based schedule separately from external perimeter testing.
