# Cybersecurity Talent Development

**SSK Controls:** 04-1  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA's cybersecurity capability is maintained through the Director of IT & MIS's ongoing professional development and through the DIS Computers managed service relationship. The Director of IT & MIS holds current Microsoft certifications (MS-102, SC-300) and engages continuously with Microsoft Tech Community, Defender product blogs, and security webinars. Cloud security and identity governance are the two priority skill domains for FY2026 development.

DIS Computers provides supplemental depth across endpoint security, network security, firewall management, and incident response. DIS personnel hold relevant industry certifications and are expected to maintain current knowledge of the platforms they manage for MCNA.

The IT & MIS function evaluates cybersecurity skill gaps annually in conjunction with the IT roadmap planning cycle. Where internal capability is insufficient for a specific risk domain, third-party expertise is engaged through DIS or directly.
