# Phishing Readiness and Email-Based Attack Response

**SSK Controls:** 04-4  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA runs phishing simulation campaigns at least annually using Microsoft Attack Simulator (Defender for Office 365). Simulations test employee recognition of phishing, credential harvesting, and business email compromise lures. Employees who fail simulations are automatically enrolled in targeted remediation training. Results are reviewed by the Director of IT & MIS to identify trends and inform annual security awareness training content updates.

At the technical layer, Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Safe Links and Safe Attachments are enforced tenant-wide, rewriting and scanning URLs and attachments in real time. Exchange Online Protection provides anti-spam, anti-phishing, and anti-malware filtering as the primary inbound mail defense layer.

Employees report suspected phishing via the Microsoft Report Message add-in in Outlook. Reported messages are reviewed by the Director of IT & MIS and triaged using Defender for Office 365 Threat Explorer.
