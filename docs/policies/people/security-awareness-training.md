# Security Awareness Training

**SSK Controls:** 04-3  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

All MCNA employees complete security awareness training annually. Training is delivered through Microsoft Viva Learning or a third-party training platform integrated with M365, covering phishing recognition, password hygiene, acceptable use, data handling, and social engineering. New employees complete security awareness training as part of onboarding before being granted access to production systems.

Completion is tracked by IT & MIS. Non-completion within the required window is escalated to department managers. The Director of IT & MIS reviews training content annually and adjusts topics to reflect current threat trends identified through CISA advisories and Defender telemetry.

Training records are retained in the HR system and the IT & MIS SharePoint library.
