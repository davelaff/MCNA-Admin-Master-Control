# Supply Chain and Third-Party Security

**SSK Controls:** 03-1, 03-2, 03-3, 03-4  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

## Scope of controls across group companies (03-1)

MCNA operates as a subsidiary of NOF Corporation. Information security controls implemented by the Director of IT & MIS apply to all MCNA systems and users in the nofmetalcoatings.us tenant. NOF Corporation IS team is engaged for incidents meeting the NOF escalation threshold. MCNA conforms to NOF global IS baseline requirements where these have been communicated.

## Security assessment before contracting (03-2)

Before engaging a new technology vendor with access to MCNA systems or data, the Director of IT & MIS conducts a pre-contract security review. Review covers: authentication and access control methods, data handling and storage practices, incident notification obligations, and relevant certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) or Microsoft compliance attestations. Vendors that cannot meet minimum security requirements are not granted system access.

## Security obligations in contracts (03-3)

Contracts with third-party vendors that access MCNA systems or data include security obligations: confidentiality requirements, data handling restrictions, incident notification timelines (24-hour notification for any breach or suspected breach involving MCNA data), and right-to-audit provisions for significant engagements. DIS Computers operates under a managed services agreement that includes these obligations.

## Ongoing third-party risk management (03-4)

Third-party access to MCNA systems is provisioned through Entra ID B2B guest accounts, limited to specific resources (SharePoint sites, applications) required for the engagement. No standing privileged access is granted to third parties. Guest accounts are reviewed quarterly by the Director of IT & MIS and deprovisioned when no longer needed. DIS technicians requiring elevated access for support tasks use time-limited privileged accounts. A vendor risk register is maintained in the IT & MIS SharePoint library.
