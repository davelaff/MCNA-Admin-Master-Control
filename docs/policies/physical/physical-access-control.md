# Physical Access Control

**SSK Controls:** 05-2  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA uses keycard-based access control for secured areas including building entry, server room, and secure storage. Access lists are maintained by facilities management in coordination with IT & MIS. Server room and IT infrastructure access is reviewed by the Director of IT & MIS when personnel changes occur and at minimum quarterly. Terminated employees' physical access is revoked on the same day as system access deprovisioning.

Visitors requiring access to areas housing IT infrastructure must be escorted by an authorized employee at all times. DIS technicians requiring onsite access to IT equipment are credentialed for the duration of the engagement and deprovisioned upon completion.

Physical access logs are retained by the access control system for a minimum of 90 days and are available to the Director of IT & MIS for review.
