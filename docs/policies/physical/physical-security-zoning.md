# Physical Security Zoning

**SSK Controls:** 05-1  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA facilities are zoned by asset sensitivity. The server room and network equipment areas are physically separated from general production and office space and require keycard or key access. Access to these areas is restricted to authorized IT personnel (the Director of IT & MIS and designated DIS technicians). The production floor, receiving, and administrative areas are zoned separately with access controlled by facility security.

Sensitive IT assets (servers, network gear, backup media) are located in physically secured areas not accessible to the general employee population. Security cameras cover facility entry points and the server room. Physical access logs are reviewed by the Director of IT & MIS when incidents warrant.

Zone boundaries and access requirements are reviewed annually and updated when facility layout or IT infrastructure changes occur.
