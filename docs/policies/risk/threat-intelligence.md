# Threat Intelligence Process

**SSK Controls:** 02-3  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA consumes threat intelligence through Microsoft's integrated security stack: Defender Threat Intelligence, Microsoft Secure Score recommendations, and Microsoft 365 Defender incident alerts. The Director of IT & MIS reviews the M365 Security Center weekly and subscribes to CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog updates and MS-ISAC advisories relevant to manufacturing sector threats.

DIS Computers monitors vendor security bulletins for MCNA's infrastructure stack (firewall, switches, endpoints) and escalates relevant threats to the Director of IT & MIS. Threat intelligence findings drive firewall rule updates, endpoint policy changes, and priority assignments in MCNA's vulnerability management process.

No standalone threat intelligence platform is deployed. Microsoft's integrated telemetry across Entra ID, Defender for Endpoint, and Exchange Online Protection provides the primary threat visibility layer for MCNA's size and risk profile.
