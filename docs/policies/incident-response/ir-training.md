# Incident Response Training

**SSK Controls:** 20-1, 20-2  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

## IR team training (20-1)

The Director of IT & MIS maintains current knowledge of incident response practices through Microsoft security certifications (SC-200 target), Microsoft Defender XDR product training, and regular review of Microsoft Incident Response playbooks and CISA incident response guidance. DIS Computers personnel hold certifications relevant to incident response (e.g., CompTIA Security+, vendor certifications) and maintain current knowledge of the platforms they manage for MCNA.

The IR team (the Director of IT & MIS + DIS) conducts tabletop exercises at minimum annually. Exercises test IR runbook procedures against realistic scenarios — ransomware, BEC, account compromise, or data exfiltration — appropriate to MCNA's threat profile. Exercise outcomes are documented and used to update runbooks and identify training gaps.

## Cross-functional IR training (20-2)

Cross-functional participants — HR, Finance, Operations management — are included in IR tabletop exercises when the scenario involves broader business impact (ransomware affecting operations, BEC targeting Finance, regulatory notification obligations). Participation ensures these functions understand their roles in the IR process: evidence preservation, communication management, regulatory response, and business continuity activation.

Cross-functional exercise outcomes are documented alongside IR team outcomes in the post-exercise report, retained in the IT & MIS SharePoint library.
