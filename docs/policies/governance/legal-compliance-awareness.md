# Legal Compliance and Regulatory Awareness

**SSK Controls:** 01-2  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA tracks applicable cybersecurity and information security laws and guidelines through the IT & MIS function. Relevant regulatory requirements — including NIST CSF guidance, CISA advisories, state data breach notification obligations, and NOF Corporation global IS requirements — are monitored by the Director of IT & MIS and reviewed for applicability to MCNA operations. Material changes are communicated to executive management and incorporated into policy revisions.

DIS Computers provides supplemental monitoring of regulatory and vendor security advisories relevant to MCNA's infrastructure. Microsoft 365 Compliance Manager and Microsoft Service Trust Portal are used to assess Microsoft's compliance posture for services MCNA depends on.

Compliance obligations identified as applicable to MCNA are documented in the IT & MIS SharePoint library and reviewed annually or when regulatory changes occur.
