# Overarching Information Security Policy

**SSK Controls:** 01-3  
**Owner:** Director of IT & MIS  
**Date:** 2026-04-26  
**Review cycle:** Annual

MCNA's information security policy establishes that all company data must remain within MCNA-controlled systems and that access requires authentication via Microsoft Entra ID. Security controls are operationalized through Conditional Access policies, Microsoft Intune device management, and Microsoft Defender across endpoints, email, and cloud services. The policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to MCNA systems.

The Director of IT & MIS owns the security policy and is responsible for its implementation, communication, and annual review. Policy updates are reviewed with executive management and communicated to all staff. The policy is aligned with NOF Corporation global information security requirements and conforms to NIST Cybersecurity Framework principles.

Employees are required to comply with the security policy as a condition of employment. Violations are handled through the HR disciplinary process. The policy is available to all employees in the MCNA SharePoint intranet.
