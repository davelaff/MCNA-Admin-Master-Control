## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Inventory of software assets is taken periodically

## Control Statement

Define rules for managing software assets (such as operating systems (OS's) and applications), and maintain their current inventory in lists, or databases; include information about configuration and licenses

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Vulnerabilities in software installed in your organization environment may not be detected and may be left vulnerable to malware or other threats. Unauthorized use of software, if undetected, cannot be remedied, possibly inviting criminal penalties due to copyright infringement etc. or claims for damages.
