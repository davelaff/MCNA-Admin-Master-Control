## Insufficient Measures Risks

The Organization may not recognize how its security posture and countermeasures fall short of the prevailing trends in cyber threats; which could result in exploits and actual impact from cyberattacks. Leveraging the knowledge and know-how based on analyses of attack patterns etc. already conducted by other companies can help prevent or control time, cost, and effort in duplicate efforts within your organization.
