## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 17-2-a | For each nonfunctional and functional requirement of the system, understand and formalize the viewpoints to be checked at the design stage according to risk and business importance | not_started | — |
| 17-2-b | Understand, starting from the planning phase in the development lifecycle, the system objectives and the information assets that should be protected from, and establish strategies to respond to risk | not_started | — |
| 17-2-c | Establish a baseline configuration in accordance with system design principles such as the principle of developing for only the essential functionality | not_started | — |
| 17-2-d | Enforce secure design principles, such as adhering to the principle of least privilege (PoLP) and ensuring that user input is validated | not_started | — |
| 17-2-e | For each system, evaluate the business impact of system downtime in the event of a failure, and implement load balancing and redundancy in implementations to achieve the length of the acceptable downtime | not_started | — |
| 17-2-f | Confidential information stored in reusable resources, such as memory, disk, or their caches, should be erased immediately when no longer needed | not_started | — |
| 17-2-g | Identify metrics to be monitored, such as CPU usage, and configure appropriate alerts | not_started | — |
| 17-2-h | Design a system, with due consideration to information security, following the viewpoints and methodology identified in the above organization stage | not_started | — |
| 17-2-i | Conduct security reviews at the system design stage, and issue instructions for improvements, as needed, such as refinement or a review of the design, considering alternatives to meet security requirements, tentative or permanent countermeasure as necessary | not_started | — |
| 17-2-j | When issuing requests for improvements stated above, reassess and confirm its implementation | not_started | — |
| 17-2-k | Create system architecture diagrams to visualize communication and data flows | not_started | — |
| 17-2-l | Regularly review the information security and cybersecurity aspects when designing information systems | not_started | — |
| 17-2-m | Regularly update system architecture diagrams | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Inappropriate or insufficient consideration to information security during the design stage may result in repetitive steps or "loopbacks" to previous stages in the process, resulting in additional costs, and potentially resulting in unprofitable development and operations.
