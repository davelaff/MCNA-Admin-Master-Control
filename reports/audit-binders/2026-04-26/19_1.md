# 19-1 — Establishing and disseminating incident response policy – detection and initial response

**Category:** 19
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Policy or procedures that direct detection and initial response are regularly reviewed

## Control Statement

Establish a policy to address incident detection and initial response, including the First Report, and its communication; obtain Management approval of this policy and disseminate to concerned parties

## Insufficient Measures Risks

In the absence of a predefined response policy, it is systemic or organized response to incidents is not possible anymore. Ad hoc responses to incidents diminish or eliminate the ability to circle back learnings from incident response and improve future incident response.
