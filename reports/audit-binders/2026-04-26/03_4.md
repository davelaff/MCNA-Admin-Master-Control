## Control Statement

## Insufficient Measures Risks

In outsourcing operations, such as outsourced IT system operations, if the demarcation of responsibilities and management functions between your company and the vendor/contractor is unclear, there is a risk of gaps in coverage, such as during an incident. Incidents within the third-party's organization may bring about secondary impact on your Organization, such as data breaches at the third party involving your Organization's data, or malware spreading from the third party’s to your Organization's.
