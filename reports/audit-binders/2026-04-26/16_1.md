## Insufficient Measures Risks

Logs entries may be incomplete in their acquisition (recording), or items subject to logs may get missed out on, which may result in delays in detection of incidents. Insufficient or deficient logs may hamper analysis of logs, resulting in a lack of ability to conduct root cause analysis on incidents and understand the impact, after an incident has occurred.
