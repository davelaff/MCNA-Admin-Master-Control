## Control Statement

Implement measures to prevent misdelivery to incorrect recipients, unauthorized sending, and spoofing of emails

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Outgoing email, if not confirmed by an approver, may lead to erroneous sending of email. Preventive controls on internal wrongdoing is not effective.
