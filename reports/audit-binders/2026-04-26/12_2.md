## Insufficient Measures Risks

Information leakage due to eavesdropping / interception of remote access communication may not be prevented. Unauthorized devices (e.g. personal devices that do not meet the security level defined by your organization and third-party devices that use stolen ID / password of executives and staff) can remotely access organization's systems. As a result, malware infection and information leakage are likely to occur. Remote access authentication may be permitted even when the initiator is using spoofed (stolen) credentials, who may be able to access unauthorized information.
