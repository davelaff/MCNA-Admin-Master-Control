## Insufficient Measures Risks

Logs or traces (of incident activity) cannot be detected by looking at log in isolation (a few at a time) or at affected systems in isolation, in an absence of a comprehensive approach to look at multiple data points may result in missing these complex incidents, and delay response to the incident.
