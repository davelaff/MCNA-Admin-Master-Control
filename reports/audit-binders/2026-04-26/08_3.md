# 08-3 — Managing access privilege

**Category:** 08
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** It is periodically checked that access privileges have been allotted correctly

## Control Statement

Establish policy to assign account access privilege and manage access privilege

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 08-3-a | Assign access privileges to an account following the defined access rights assignment process | not_started | — |
| 08-3-b | Allow or restrict access to each piece of information and to each system, matching with the access privilege | not_started | — |
| 08-3-c | Establish access control policies and allocation processes based on the principle of least privilege (PoLP) and separation of duties (SoD), also called segregation of duties | not_started | — |
| 08-3-d | Ensure that access privileges of employees' accounts are updated immediately in response to events such as separation (termination) of employment relationship or transfer, such as by integrating with the HR database or people administration system; employment events could include all workers in general, and transfer could include transfer of roles, teams, or other human resource transfer | not_started | — |
| 08-3-e | Regularly conduct inventory and review of access privilege (i.e. periodic audit) | not_started | — |
| 08-3-f | Conduct an inventory of access privileges regularly (at least once every quarter) | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Accounts not intended to access that information may be able to access it, or accounts dormant or unused for a long time or obsolete accounts may continue to access accounts, potentially leading to information leaks.
