## Insufficient Measures Risks

As internal and external factors for an organization change, its business models, importance of any information retained, and security requirements will also change accordingly. However, if existing security measures do not adapt to these changes, the measures will likely become obsolete. Implementation status of security measures and any unaddressed risks cannot be tracked fully anymore. As a result, optimal security measures can no longer be implemented.
