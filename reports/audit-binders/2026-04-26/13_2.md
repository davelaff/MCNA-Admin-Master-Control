# 13-2 — Management of wireless access points

**Category:** 13
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Periodic review of wireless access point installation and management method is conducted

## Control Statement

Manage the wireless access points that are allowed to connect to the network and ensure that no unauthorized wireless access points are connected

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 13-2-a | Maintain, using a list/database/ledger, and manage wireless access points allowed to connect to your organization's network | not_started | — |
| 13-2-b | Restrict access to the corporate network(s) if providing wireless access points to devices outside of your control, such as personal devices of employees or visitor devices | not_started | — |
| 13-2-c | Detect that an unauthorized wireless access point is connected to the network and take measures such as removing it | not_started | — |
| 13-2-d | Deploy wireless intrusion detection/prevention systems (WIDS/WIPS) to automatically detect and respond to unauthorized wireless access points | not_started | — |
| 13-2-e | Periodic review of wireless access point installation and management method is conducted | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Connections to unauthorized access points may lead to eavesdropping and interceptions of internal communications, information leaks.
