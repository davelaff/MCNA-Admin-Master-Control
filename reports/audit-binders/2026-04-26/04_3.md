## Insufficient Measures Risks

The following incidents are likely to occur due to insufficient awareness and skill of officers and employees regarding security. Malware infection from suspicious mails and malicious sites. Internal fraud by retirees and managers (bringing out or sending out of information from company environment, in an unauthorized manner etc.). Information leak due to erroneous transmission of mail.
