# 05-2 — Implementing physical access and entry control

**Category:** 05
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** The authentication method is reviewed on a regular basis

## Control Statement

Use means of authentication of a rigor appropriate to an area's physical security segregation zone or grade to control entry and exit, and obtain and preserve authentication record trail

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 05-2-a | Implement appropriate authentication for entering and leaving facilities, based on the security level classification of the location | not_started | — |
| 05-2-b | Safely manage keys and IC cards, and promptly collect or invalidate unnecessary ones | not_started | — |
| 05-2-c | Keep a log of entry and exit records at important facilities and areas | not_started | — |
| 05-2-d | For resources kept under a lock-and-key, retain access records for issuing (granting) the keys, and in the case of IC cards or biometric authentication, keep a log of access records and retain them for a set period | not_started | — |
| 05-2-e | Periodically audit the access records to ensure that there is no unauthorized access | not_started | — |
| 05-2-f | Regularly review lock-and-key management system and authentication methods | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Unauthorized intrusions, information leaks, and theft may occur in locations of high importance. Further, there is no trace of such fraud and thus it may not be detectable.
