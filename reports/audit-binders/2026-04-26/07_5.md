## Insufficient Measures Risks

Proper response to vulnerabilities cannot be implemented, and dangerous vulnerability persists, making the device/software etc. susceptible to malware. When a patch is applied without sufficient testing of its operation in a test environment or the like, unexpected system suspensions, or operational malfunctions are likely to occur.
