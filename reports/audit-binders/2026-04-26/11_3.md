## Insufficient Measures Risks

Unauthorized communication from the outside cannot be detected or blocked and unauthorized or improper access to internal systems may occur. In the event of malware infection, communication with the C&C servers (command and control server, a machine under the control of a malicious actor or hacker and used to direct or execute cyberattacks) on the Internet cannot be stopped.
