## Insufficient Measures Risks

Hardware assets (PC, mobile, external storage media, etc.) that are stolen, lost or taken out in an unauthorized or improper manner cannot be tracked/traced. When unnecessary hardware assets are left unattended on the company network, malware infections may occur in those devices.
