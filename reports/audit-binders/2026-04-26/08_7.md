## Insufficient Measures Risks

Without appropriate oversight, control, and logging, when fraudulent activity using a privileged ID occurs, it may not be possible to detect it. Without appropriate tracking and record keeping of the operation, it may not be possible to investigate the operation ex post facto and insufficient information to perform an adequate investigation.
