# 10-2 — Implementation of endpoint log collection and immediate response to malware infection (e.g. EDR product/solution)

**Category:** 10
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** The details and methodology of the response are reviewed on a regular basis

## Control Statement

Implement products to acquire endpoint logs, to actively detect threats, to visualize suspicious behavior and respond in real time when malware infection occurs (e.g. EDR solution)

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 10-2-a | Implement logging at endpoints with a granularity and level of detail sufficient for tracking malicious programs, when a malware infection is detected and must be tracked (examples of actions to be logged: read or write out (print) operations on files, operations on the OS registry, processes started, access to networks etc.) | not_started | — |
| 10-2-b | Obtain command-line audit logs (such as PowerShell or BASH) for the device, equipment, or other machines applicable | not_started | — |
| 10-2-c | Implement mechanisms to prevent or preempt operations by malware (malicious or unauthorized communication, unauthorized encrypted communication, executing malicious programs, etc.) | not_started | — |
| 10-2-d | Implement mechanisms to automatically restore the machine and its state to their proper state, such as by deleting unauthorized programs) | not_started | — |
| 10-2-e | Regularly simulate logging status, and timely and swift response to malware infection on devices | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Malware infection may not be prevented. In the event of an actual malware infection, it might be hard to detect and stop the spread of infection in a timely and swift manner.
