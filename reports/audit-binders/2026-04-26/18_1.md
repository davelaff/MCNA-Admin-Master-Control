**Regularly Reviewed:** Periodic training assuming accidents and disasters is conducted and readiness is reviewed

## Control Statement

Determine important IT systems for the company and prepare for continuation and early recovery of those services in case of accident or disaster occur

## Insufficient Measures Risks

In the absence of insufficient or improper disaster preparedness and advance planning, prompt response to suspension of business activities due to natural disasters or cyber-attacks etc. is made difficult, delaying recovery of business activities.
