# 06-5 — Understand network configuration

**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Whether or not there are discrepancies between the actual situation and the configuration chart is periodically checked

## Control Statement

Create a network diagram of your company and grasp all communications and data related to company's business

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 06-5-a | Create a diagram showing connections between network devices and systems (this could be variably referred to as a network diagram, a network configuration diagram, a network configuration state and flow diagram) | not_started | — |
| 06-5-b | Understand all business-related communications and data flows between users and systems | not_started | — |
| 06-5-c | For each network device or equipment, manage a record of its configuration rules, such as permitted communication (inbound/outbound), and the business justification to support or justify this | not_started | — |
| 06-5-d | Regularly check that there is no difference between the configuration diagram (mentioned above) and the actual device configuration or data flow | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

If the network configuration is not well understood, unexpected misconfiguration or unauthorized changes can lead to unauthorized or improper access to information from outside the organization.
