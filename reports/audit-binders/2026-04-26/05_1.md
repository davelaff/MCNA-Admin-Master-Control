# 05-1 — Implementing physical security zoning by asset sensitivity

**Category:** 05
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Periodic review of the physical security levels and the scope of locations is conducted

## Control Statement

Establish grades of physical security and zone segregation and divide physical locations into these zones accordingly. Monitor behavior of individuals entering areas classified as sensitive or important in your nomenclature.

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 05-1-a | Define required security levels and classify physical location in these, based on the intended use of the location such as working area and server storage | not_started | — |
| 05-1-b | Physically segregate access environments and locations used to access information assets For example, workspaces are separated according to the attributes, roles, permissions, and kind of information handled, and by who accesses it (employees, partner companies, outsiders) in that space etc. | not_started | — |
| 05-1-c | Physically protect designated locations and equipment (e.g., servers, cabling) from environmental threats (e.g., flooding, fire) and unauthorized access based on their importance | not_started | — |

| 05-1-e | Regularly review the security levels used for classification and the range and scope of classification for locations, in response to changes in the layout of working spaces or nature of work | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Unauthorized persons such as outsiders can easily infiltrate the locations with high importance, causing information leaks and physical theft, etc. Security classifications may not get updated properly with changes in physical layouts, resulting in leak or exposure of information to unintended locations.
