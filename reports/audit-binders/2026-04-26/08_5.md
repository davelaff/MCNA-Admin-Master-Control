# 08-5 — Monitoring and responding to unusual access

**Category:** 08
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** The definition of normal and abnormal account behavior is reviewed periodically

## Control Statement

Monitor unusual account usage, such as logging in other than business hours, and take corrective action such as account suspension if necessary

_No evidence linked._
