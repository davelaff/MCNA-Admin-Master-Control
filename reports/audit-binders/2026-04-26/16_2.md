# 16-2 — Storing, retaining, and protecting logs

**Category:** 16
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** It is regularly verified that logs are protected appropriately, and there is no fraudulent access

## Control Statement

Store logs for the required period and protect them from unauthorized access

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 16-2-a | Confirm that the storage period of the acquired log meets national laws and industry regulations, but in general retain logs for at least 12 months | not_started | — |
| 16-2-b | Check that the log storage location has sufficient storage space | not_started | — |
| 16-2-c | Retain logs for an appropriately set number of past iterations (i.e. define and store a couple of iterations over the log's history) | not_started | — |
| 16-2-d | Store logs in a location outside the system for or from which they were obtained, and/or at a remote location, geographically removed, to protect logs from tampering or unauthorized access | not_started | — |
| 16-2-e | Restrict access to logs to the fewest number of users needed | not_started | — |
| 16-2-f | Encrypt logs, if needed, based on a judgement of the importance of the information they contain | not_started | — |
| 16-2-g | Regularly verify that logs are protected appropriately, and there is no fraudulent access | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Logs may be rendered open to tampering, deletion, or disabling during cyberattacks, which may hamper or disallow forensics and root-cause investigations in the aftermath of cyberattacks.
