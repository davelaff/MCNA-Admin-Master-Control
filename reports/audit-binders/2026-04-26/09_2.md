# 09-2 — Encryption of data at rest

**Category:** 09
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** There is a regular check to see whether the encryption method has been compromised or not

## Control Statement

Encrypt data which is stored in the endpoints and servers

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 09-2-a | Identify and list the data at rest that should be encrypted, and establish policies such as encryption procedures | not_started | — |
| 09-2-b | Only use encryption methods (algorithms, protocols etc) that have been verified and certified for use in encryption of data at rest | not_started | — |
| 09-2-c | Encrypt stored data (i.e., data at rest) in accordance with defined policies | not_started | — |
| 09-2-d | Encrypt hard disks etc (storage) of devices, equipment, or servers | not_started | — |
| 09-2-e | Encrypt data stored in external storage media | not_started | — |
| 09-2-f | Encrypt backup data | not_started | — |
| 09-2-g | Hash and salt (cryptographic salting) the stored authentication information | not_started | — |
| 09-2-h | Implementing IRM / DRM (Information Rights Management, and Digital Rights Management) as necessary, to encrypt files, restrict reading and writing, and record operation history | not_started | — |
| 09-2-i | Implementing DLP (Data Loss Prevention) as necessary and carry out detection and use restrictions when data is taken out of control range | not_started | — |
| 09-2-j | Regularly check if an encryption algorithm in use is reported as compromised | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Important information in an unencrypted (plain text, etc.) state is readily available to read and extract, and results in information leaks. Leaked information cannot be deleted afterwards.
