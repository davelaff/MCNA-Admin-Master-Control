# 06-4 — Managing internal and external information systems

**Category:** 06
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Inventory of these managed information systems is kept current by reviewing it periodically

## Control Statement

Maintain a current inventory of information systems (including cloud services, and systems managed/owned by individual business units) in lists or databases; include purpose of use and indicate presence or absence of internet exposure

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 06-4-a | Define internal and external systems (including cloud services) that your organization is responsible for managing | not_started | — |
| 06-4-b | Identify and track all information systems (e.g., business systems) used within your organization | not_started | — |
| 06-4-c | If your organization provides a service or services to customers, identify all systems involved in this activity as such | not_started | — |
| 06-4-d | Define the importance of systems based on internal factors (e.g., business impact) and external factors (e.g., impact on customers during outages) | not_started | — |
| 06-4-e | Identify, track and keep a current inventory of all information systems and external systems used by your organization | not_started | — |
| 06-4-f | Manage identified systems in an inventory/list/ledger with information on managing or owning department, purpose of use, configuration-related information (on-premises/cloud), internet connectivity, FQDN, and IP addresses | not_started | — |
| 06-4-g | Regularly validate the information by auditing the system and its metadata/attributes against its entry within the asset management inventory, and update the inventory information in the list/ledger/database if needed | not_started | — |
| 06-4-h | Regularly search or search the environment for unmanaged or unrecognized systems to maintain inventory accuracy and completeness | not_started | — |
| 06-4-i | Regularly review the criteria for defining system criticality and the criticality assigned to each system | not_started | — |
