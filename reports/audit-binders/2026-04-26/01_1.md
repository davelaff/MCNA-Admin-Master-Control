## Insufficient Measures Risks

Inappropriate or sub-optimal allocation of management resources (people, things, and money) may result in waste of these resources and hinder efficient execution of security response and system. Failure to align with other risk management systems (i.e. risk management systems other than IT security/cyber risk) within the organization can lead to inconsistency in risk management and across the organization. During an incident, such as an information breach, the command-and-control system may find itself in disarray, making it hard to respond promptly and appropriately.
