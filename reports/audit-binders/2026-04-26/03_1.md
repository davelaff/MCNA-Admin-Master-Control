# 03-1 — Enforcing cybersecurity- and information security controls across all group companies

**Category:** 03
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Regularly grasped the status of the security measures of the entire group and measures

## Control Statement

Understand the security posture and current state of cybersecurity and information security measures across all group companies, including subsidiaries and affiliated entities; implement countermeasures shared or enforced groupwide

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Group companies cannot deter incidents such as information breach or fraud, resulting in increased damage to the whole conglomerate and shared brand identity, leading to social credibility loss and falling stock prices.
