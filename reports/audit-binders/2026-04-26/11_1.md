# 11-1 — Network segmentation

**Category:** 11
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** The Organization network is regularly reviewed to validate that segmentation is operating as expected, and as stated in the design

## Control Statement

Enforce network segmentation that is appropriate to your organization’s structuring and operations

## Evidence

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 11-1-a | Prepare the segmentation design of your network so that it is appropriate to your organization’s structuring and business operations | not_started | — |
| 11-1-b | Design segments to allow the minimum two-way communication required | not_started | — |
| 11-1-c | Segregate the data plane (communication traffic) from the control plane | not_started | — |
| 11-1-d | Demarcate segments at the level of business unit (organization unit, department, etc.) | not_started | — |
| 11-1-e | Design segments by assuming that malicious actors have gained capability of lateral movement (that involve attackers or malware spread across the breadth of the internal network), so that such segments allow containment and protection during an actual intrusion | not_started | — |
| 11-1-f | Roll out the actual physical/logical network segmentation, based on the designed network architecture | not_started | — |
| 11-1-g | Perform network management operations in an isolated environment or device separate from the main network | not_started | — |
| 11-1-h | Based on risk and operational management load, implement host-level segmentation or micro-segmentation as needed | not_started | — |
| 11-1-i | Ensure that only authorized systems can communicate across isolated network segments | not_started | — |
| 11-1-j | Regularly review and monitor the operating status and effectiveness of network segmentation, and adjust or improve as needed | not_started | — |

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Unintended actors, or users/actors not originally intended to have access, may be able to access information. In an unsegregated environment, malware infection can spread across the organizations via the network, to other devices and departments.
