# 03-3 — Specifying security obligations within third party contracts

**Category:** 03
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** The recorded content is regularly reviewed

## Control Statement

Delineate and document roles, requirements and delineate scope pertaining to cybersecurity within third-party contracts (e.g., commercial partners, vendors, subcontractors)

_No evidence linked._

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
