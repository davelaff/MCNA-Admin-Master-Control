## Insufficient Measures Risks

Information leaks to the Internet services (cloud storage, social media, bulletin board, web mail, etc.). Malware infection occurs due to access to a malicious site loaded with malware. In the event of malware infection, communication with the C&C servers (command and control server, a machine under the control of a malicious actor or hacker and used to direct or execute cyberattacks) on the Internet cannot be stopped.
