## Insufficient Measures Risks

Incoming emails with malware attachments may be introduced to the organization, and an infection caused by it may not be prevented. Incoming emails attached with executables or other file types often used to distribute malware (bat, exe, vbs, lnk etc), may not be preempted. Without the ability to verify the safety of the URLs present in the body of an email, it is possible for the organization's user to be lured to suspicious or malicious websites. Malware-attached email reaches users before the pattern file (malware definitions or spam registry) is updated, and malware infection cannot be prevented by antivirus / spam mail filter.
