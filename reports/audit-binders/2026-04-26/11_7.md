# 11-7 — Response to abnormal increase in traffic

**Category:** 11
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed

## Control Statement

Establish a policy for detecting and responding to abnormal increases in communication traffic.

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Higher traffic levels may go undetected, resulting in network or service suspensions. DDoS attack may not be detected, and response could be delayed or lead to system/service suspension.
