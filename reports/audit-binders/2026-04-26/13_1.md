## MCNA Position

## Control Statement

Establish policy for wireless communication usage, manage the devices that allow wireless communication, encrypt wireless communication data by strong encryption method

## Insufficient Measures Risks

Eavesdropping / interception of wireless communication may be possible, resulting in possible information leaks. Remote access authentication may be permitted even when the initiator is using spoofed (stolen) credentials, who may be able to access unauthorized information.
