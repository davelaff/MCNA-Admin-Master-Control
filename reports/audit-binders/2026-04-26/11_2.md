# 11-2 — Denying communication which is not permitted

**Category:** 11
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** The definitions of unauthorized communication are reviewed on a regular basis

## Control Statement

Block connection from unauthorized IP address or port to internal and/or external network

_No evidence linked._
