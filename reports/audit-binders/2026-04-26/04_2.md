## Insufficient Measures Risks

New employees may not understand their security responsibilities, resulting in an increased security risk to the organization. Failure to sign a valid nondisclosure agreement for a departing employee will increase the possibility of divulging of organization's sensitive information (such as trade secrets) to competitors. No disciplinary action can be taken against workers in violation of security policies or rules. In addition, if the rules and penalties are not made known, security violation cannot be prevented.
