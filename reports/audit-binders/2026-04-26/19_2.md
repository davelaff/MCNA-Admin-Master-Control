# 19-2 — Establishing and disseminating incident response policy – recovery and improvement

**Category:** 19
**Source version:** 2026-01-01
**Current maturity:** not_regularly_reviewed
**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed
**Last reviewed:** never
**Next review due:** not scheduled

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Policies or procedures that direct recovery and improvement (including the prevention of recurrence) are regularly reviewed

## Control Statement

Establish a policy on incident recovery and improvement, including the Second Report or the "First Follow-up" Report and all subsequent reporting, and their sharing and communication; obtain Management approval of this policy and disseminate to concerned parties

## Insufficient Measures Risks

In the absence of a predefined response policy, it is systemic or organized response to incidents is not possible anymore. Ad hoc responses to incidents diminish or eliminates the ability to circle back learnings from incident response and improve future incident response.
