## Insufficient Measures Risks

Insufficient oversight on the use of unauthorized use of cloud service (shadow IT), may result in improper use without the organization knowing of it. Insufficient oversight on the content of communication to cloud services may result in spread of malware infections or information leaks/data leaks.
