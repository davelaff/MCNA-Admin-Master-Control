# 08-1 — Creating and provisioning access and accounts, and conducting access inventory

**Category:** 08
**Source version:** 2026-01-01

**Target maturity:** Regularly Reviewed

## MCNA Position

**Regularly Reviewed:** Accounts are reviewed periodically through an inventory process

## Control Statement

Set a process for account management and manage it according to the processes you have established

## Recommended Actions

| ID | Action | Status | Owner |
|----|--------|--------|-------|
| 08-1-a | Issue accounts adhering to the established protocol, policy, or procedure governing new accounts issuing or provision | not_started | — |
| 08-1-b | Issue accounts in line with security requirement level (privileged access, high-level access, general access, etc) | not_started | — |
| 08-1-c | Abolish use of shared ID as much as possible and manage all access through individual accounts (ie accounts per individual) | not_started | — |
| 08-1-d | Set an expiration date for all issued accounts, at the time of issuing | not_started | — |
| 08-1-e | In order to uniquely identify the user, reuse of the same ID is restricted for a certain period after the account ID is destroyed | not_started | — |
| 08-1-f | Promptly delete or disable accounts following the user's (employee, etc.) HR transfer (e.g., of roles/departments etc.) or separation (resignation, termination, etc.), such as over a sync with the connected HRM, or other source of truth | not_started | — |
| 08-1-g | Automatically disable or remove accounts that are not used for a period of time | not_started | — |
| 08-1-h | To maintain an audit trail, when deleting an account, deactivate the account for a period of time before fully deleting the account | not_started | — |
| 08-1-i | Maintain a list of user accounts and periodically perform inventory of accounts | not_started | — |
| 08-1-j | Conduct account inventory regularly (at least once every quarter) | not_started | — |
